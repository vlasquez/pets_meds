# Release notes — v1.0.0

Both texts are under Play's 500-character limit and work for the
App Store "What's New" field too.

## English (en-US)

Welcome to Pet Meds! 🐾

First release:
• Pet profiles with photo, breed, age and weight history
• Medication catalog and treatments per pet
• Flexible schedules: daily, every X hours/days/months, weekdays, cycles, or on demand
• Today view with per-hour intake checks and progress bars
• Day-by-day progress history with retroactive corrections
• Vaccination log with renewal reminders
• Local notifications — everything stays on your device
• Light/dark theme, English and Spanish

## Español (es-419 / es-ES)

¡Bienvenido a Pet Meds! 🐾
